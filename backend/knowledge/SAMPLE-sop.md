# SAMPLE SOP (invented for the demo; replace with the site's real procedures)

# Fire in Block B
Press the nearest red fire alarm. Do not use the lifts. Leave by stairwell 2 and gather at the main gate
assembly point. The Head Guard takes the roll call at the assembly point. Only the Head Guard or the Admin
decides to call the fire station.

# Person down
Do not move the person unless they are in danger. Call the Head Guard, then bring the first-aid kit from
the security cabin. Keep the area clear until help arrives.

# Unattended bag
Do not touch or open it. Keep people 10 metres away. Call the Head Guard, who checks the camera clip for
the owner before deciding anything else.

# Restricted area entry
The server room door and the loading bay are restricted. A guard goes to the area, checks the person's ID
badge and escorts them out if they are not authorised.
